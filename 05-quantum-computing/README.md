# 05 — Quantum Computing & Quantum AI

Study notebooks, mathematical derivations, and quantum circuit simulations (Qiskit / PennyLane) covering **Principles of Quantum Mechanics, Qubits, and Quantum Algorithms** as part of my PhD preparation in Quantum Computing and AI.

## Focus Areas

### 1. Principles of Quantum Mechanics & Qubits
- Postulates of quantum mechanics, Hilbert spaces, Dirac (bra-ket) notation, and density matrices.
- Single- and multi-qubit systems: superposition, tensor products, quantum entanglement, and measurement.
- Quantum noise, decoherence, and basic quantum error correction.

### 2. Quantum Algorithms & Circuits
- Reversible computing and universal quantum gate sets (Pauli, Hadamard, CNOT, phase gates).
- Foundational quantum algorithms: Deutsch-Jozsa, Bernstein-Vazirani, Quantum Fourier Transform (QFT), Shor's algorithm, and Grover's search.
- Quantum-classical hybrid algorithms and Quantum Machine Learning (QML): Variational Quantum Eigensolvers (VQE), QAOA, and parameterized quantum circuits.

## Reference Library

### Mathematical & Algorithmic Prerequisites (Current Library)
- ***Mathematics for Machine Learning*** — Marc Peter Deisenroth, A. Aldo Faisal & Cheng Soon Ong
  *Core foundation for complex vector spaces, inner products, eigenvalues/eigenvectors, and unitary transformations.*
- ***Introduction to Algorithms* (CLRS)** — Thomas H. Cormen
  *Core foundation for classical complexity classes ($P$, $NP$) and asymptotic speedups.*

### Recommended Additions (Core PhD Track)
- ***Quantum Computation and Quantum Information*** — Michael A. Nielsen & Isaac L. Chuang
  *The standard PhD-level reference ("Mike & Ike") covering quantum mechanics, circuits, algorithms, and information theory.*
- ***Quantum Computing: An Applied Approach*** — Jack D. Hidary
  *Hands-on reference bridging linear algebra with Python implementations and quantum circuits.*
- ***Machine Learning with Quantum Computers*** — Maria Schuld & Francesco Petruccione
  *Essential PhD research reference connecting quantum computing with kernel methods, neural networks, and variational circuits.*
- ***Dancing with Qubits*** — Robert S. Sutor
  *Accessible conceptual and mathematical bridge into qubits, gates, and quantum algorithms.*
